# Garco WhatsApp Sales Assistant — User Manual

## Getting Started

**Access the dashboard:** Open your browser and go to the dashboard URL provided by your admin.

**Log in:** Enter your email and password, then click **Sign In**.

---

## Dashboard (Home)

The dashboard shows what's happening right now:

- **Active Conversations** — customers currently chatting
- **Pending Handoffs** — conversations needing a human
- **Quote Requests** — quotes sent to customers
- **Appointments** — upcoming scheduled visits
- **AI Conversations** — chats the AI is handling
- **New Customers** — total active customers

Click any number to jump to that section. The **Attention Required** banner appears when conversations need your help.

---

## Inbox

The inbox is where you manage customer conversations.

**Layout:**
- **Left panel** — list of conversations with filters (All, Active, Waiting, Closed)
- **Center** — message thread with the customer
- **Right panel** — customer details and conversation status

**What you can do:**
- Read the full conversation history
- See which messages are from the **AI Assistant** vs the customer
- Send a message to the customer
- Change conversation status (Mark as Waiting, Mark Resolved)
- View the customer's profile

**Understanding message labels:**
- **Customer** — message from the WhatsApp user
- **AI Assistant** — response generated by the AI
- **Staff** — message sent by a team member

---

## Customers

View all customers who have contacted you via WhatsApp.

- Click any customer to see their full profile
- Search by name or phone number
- Filter by status (Active, Blocked, Archived)

**Customer profile shows:**
- Contact details (name, phone, email, company)
- Tags and notes
- Conversation history
- First/last seen dates

---

## Leads

The **Sales Pipeline** shows where customers are in the buying process.

Two views:
- **Pipeline** — visual columns by stage (New → Engaged → Qualified → Closed)
- **List** — table view of all leads

Click any lead to open the conversation.

---

## Quotes

Track all quotes sent to customers.

- Filter by status: Draft, Sent, Accepted, Rejected, Expired
- Click a quote to see full details (line items, totals, notes)

---

## Appointments

Manage scheduled customer appointments.

- View all appointments with date, time, and status
- Filter by status: Scheduled, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled
- Click to see appointment details

---

## Knowledge Base

This is the information the AI uses to answer customer questions.

- View all knowledge documents
- See document status (Pending, Processing, Indexed, Failed)
- Search and preview content

**Note:** Only admins should add or edit knowledge documents. The AI relies on this information to give accurate responses.

---

## Handoffs

Handoffs are conversations the AI has flagged for a human to handle.

**You'll see handoffs when:**
- A customer explicitly asks for a person
- The AI is unsure how to respond
- The situation requires human judgment

**What to do:**
1. Click **Open** to view the conversation
2. Read what's been said
3. Click **Accept** to take over
4. Respond to the customer
5. Click **Resolve** when done

---

## AI Activity

Monitor how the AI is responding to customers.

- Select a conversation to see all AI responses
- Each AI response shows the customer's original message for context
- Useful for training and quality assurance

---

## Analytics

View sales and conversation metrics:

- Total conversations and messages
- Conversion rate (quotes accepted vs sent)
- Pipeline value
- Conversation volume over time
- Handoffs by reason

Use the date range selector (7, 30, 90 days) to adjust the period.

---

## Settings

**General** — company settings and preferences

**Staff** — view team members and their roles

**WhatsApp** — connection status indicator

**System** — system-level settings (admin only)

---

## Key Things to Remember

1. **AI vs Human** — The dashboard clearly labels AI-generated messages. Always check the label before responding.

2. **Handoffs need action** — When the Attention Required banner appears, check the Handoffs page.

3. **Customer context** — The right panel in conversations shows everything known about the customer.

4. **Status matters** — Keep conversation statuses updated so the team knows what needs attention.

5. **Knowledge is power** — The AI uses the Knowledge Base to answer questions. Keep it accurate and up to date.
